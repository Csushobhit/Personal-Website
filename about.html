I’m drawn to problems that involve building systems from the ground up. Engineering wasn’t always a clear decision, but it grew on me over time. My parents never forced it, though somewhere along the way they shaped this image in my mind that engineers build things that matter. As I explored it more, I found myself drawn to problem solving—especially in AI. At the same time, I understood that strong systems matter just as much, which led me to explore backend development alongside it, building a more balanced foundation.

These little breaks help me reset and think differently about problems and people. And just to be clear, enjoying these things doesn’t necessarily mean I’m good at them — I mostly just show up, try, and have fun (with very questionable results).

I’m still early in my journey, but I value building things that are thoughtful, efficient, and grounded in real use. For me, progress comes from consistently learning, refining ideas, and turning them into something meaningful over time.
